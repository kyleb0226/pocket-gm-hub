Your saves

Back up every franchise from all three games to one file, or restore a backup — including one exported from the old single-game apps. Restoring adds careers; it never replaces one that's already here.

Put it on your home screen

Each game installs as its own app with its own icon — open the game first, then add it. Adding this page instead gives you a single icon that opens the menu.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Installed — look for the Pocket GM icon on your home screen.
